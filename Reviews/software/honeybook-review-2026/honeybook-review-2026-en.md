## Core Features: One Pipeline, Not a Patchwork

Everything below lives in the same backend and talks to each other. The one I want to single out first is **Smart Files** — HoneyBook's flagship. It's not "attach a PDF, attach a contract, attach an invoice." It's one elegant, page-like file where the client reviews your offer, e-signs, and pays the deposit in a single click.

- **Smart Files**: proposal, contract, and invoice fused into one interactive page. This is the feature that separates HoneyBook from a traditional CRM.
- **Lead forms**: embed them on your site or socials; new inquiries land in the system automatically.
- **Automated workflows**: a form submission can trigger a reply email, a booking link, and a contract reminder without you touching anything.
- **Scheduler**: built-in booking that syncs with Google and Outlook calendars — plenty of users drop Calendly entirely.
- **Invoicing & payments**: credit card, ACH, Apple Pay, Google Pay; automatic reminders, payment plans, late fees, autopay. Card processing runs **2.9% + $0.25** per transaction.
- **Client portal**: every client gets a page showing project status, signed documents, and outstanding invoices.
- **HoneyBook AI**: a chat-style assistant that drafts follow-ups and writes copy for you.

Integrations include QuickBooks, Zapier, Calendly, Zoom, Gmail, Google Calendar, Canva, Flodesk, and Pic-Time.

## ⭐ The Real Story: A Referral Program Built for Circles

HoneyBook's referral design fits the "community product" model perfectly, and it runs on two parallel tracks.

### 1. Member Referral — Use the Tool, Get Paid for Recommending Peers

If you're a HoneyBook member, you get a unique referral link. That's it. Here's the structure:

- The person you refer gets **30% off their first year (or first two months on monthly billing)** — so your link is actually a favor, not a pitch.
- **You earn**: **$100** for a referral on a monthly plan, **$200** on an annual plan.
- **Step bonuses** scale with volume: +$25 per referral for referrals 6–25, +$50 for 26–50, +$75 for 51–100, +$100 beyond 100. **Hit 25+ referrals and your Essentials membership is free.** HoneyBook says the ceiling here is **$10,000**.
- Tracking is cookie-based: anyone who clicks your link and comes back within 30 days counts. Once they start a trial, **attribution locks for 180 days** — nobody swoops in and steals the credit.

> **Why this is a weapon in a circle-driven market**: HoneyBook's users are already neighbors — photographers, planners, and designers who trade recommendations constantly. Say the word in a 200-person wedding planner group and three people sign up: that's $300–$600 from one honest sentence. The better the tool, the more natural the recommendation, and the more the commission snowballs. **A software expense quietly turns into passive income.**

## Where It Falls Short (Honestly)

It's polished, but here's what you'll bump into:

- **No free tier** — there's a 30-day free trial (no card needed), but after that you're paying. Pure tire-kickers will feel the gate.
- **Annual billing is the only smart way to pay**: Essentials at $59 monthly adds up fast if you're only picking up occasional gigs.
- **Reporting is basic**: fine for a pulse check, but deep financial analysis means exporting elsewhere.
- **Template customization has limits**: branding is covered, but free-form layout hacking takes workarounds.
- **Automation branching is shallow**: simple flows are lovely; complex if/else logic isn't — power users will lean on Zapier.
- **Circle reputation cuts both ways**: because the community is tight-knit, if the tool doesn't work for you, don't push it in your circle. **The referral machine only spins when the product genuinely holds up.**
