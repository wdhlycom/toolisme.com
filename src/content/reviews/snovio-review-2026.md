## What it actually is: an all-in-one cold-email platform

One sentence: **one system runs the entire "find → verify → warm → send → CRM follow-up" pipeline.** The audience is foreign-trade sellers, B2B exporters, growth teams, and sales agencies.

Four blocks under one roof:

1. **Email Finder** — a Chrome extension that scrapes contacts and their emails directly from LinkedIn / Sales Navigator pages; you can also bulk-mine a company website for CEOs, procurement managers, and other decision-makers.
2. **Email Verifier** — built in, filters dead or abandoned addresses in real time to cut bounces and protect your domain.
3. **Drip Campaigns** — multi-branch logic ("no open after 3 days → send A; opened but no reply → send B"), AI-assisted writing, and LinkedIn automation on top.
4. **Free CRM** — replies from interested prospects flow straight into a visual pipeline, killing the need for HubSpot or Pipedrive.

## How it works: why it's both safe and fast

- **Waterfall verification.** Real-time SMTP handshake + database comparison, two passes on every address. Higher deliverability, fewer bounces — that's the core of its data quality.
- **Peer-to-peer warm-up network.** Real accounts and AI-written content exchange messages to build sender reputation. Same principle as standalone warm-up tools, but **bundled into the subscription** — which is exactly why it undercuts "Hunter + a separate warm-up tool" on cost.

## Feature by feature, including the real pitfalls

### 1. Finding and verifying (its strongest game)

LinkedIn extension scraping + bulk domain mining + built-in verification — a genuinely handy combo for B2B. One account manager at Okisam reported collecting 80,000+ leads in a month and driving down cost per lead.

**The real pitfall (data freshness):** third-party reviews repeatedly flag outdated entries — people who've left companies still sitting in the database. **Always run the verifier over a batch before importing it wholesale.**

### 2. Sequences and follow-up (good, not great)

Multi-branch logic, AI writing, unlimited follow-ups — all present. Open-rate wins are common (Populus Sales' CEO reported a jump from 25% to 73%).

![Snov.io Drip Campaign workflow canvas: branch logic with AI writing assist](/reviews/snovio-feature.png)

**The omnichannel play:** email sequences and LinkedIn automation can run in parallel — no reply on email, follow up with a LinkedIn connection request or DM. One lead, two touches. That's the line between a "sending tool" and a real acquisition platform, and it's where Snov.io separates from pure email tools.

**The real pitfall (LinkedIn costs extra):** LinkedIn automation is **not included** — it's a $69/mo per-slot add-on. If you're planning a LinkedIn DM matrix, budget for it.

### 3. The free CRM (a pleasant surprise)

Up to 20 pipelines with 100 stages each, synced with Google Calendar. As a freebie it's genuinely usable; don't expect HubSpot depth. Its real value is **native integration** — sequence replies land on the board without manual copying.

### 4. The credit system (its biggest temperament)

1 credit = 1 prospect search or 1 email verification. Search and verification draw from the **same pool** — **lower tiers burn through it fast**, and unused credits **don't roll over** (Custom Ultra excepted). It's the most-cited complaint, and it's fair: align your budget with your volume, or you'll hit the wall mid-month.

**Credit-saving tip:** dedupe before bulk-grabbing — don't search the same domain or contact twice — and verify in one batch afterward instead of as you go. These two habits noticeably cut repeat charges, and they matter most on the cheap tiers. It's the most-mentioned money-saver in real user feedback.
