## The Honest Downsides

No tool is perfect. Here's what I have to flag:

- **Entry price sits above Shopify's base tier** (€24.90 vs $29 — similar, but Shopify has cheaper entry points and WiziShop bills in EUR). Tight budgets will feel it.
- **Europe/US-centric.** It serves the Francophone + Anglophone world first; multilingual support is European languages (FR/EN/DE/IT/ES/PT/NL) with no Chinese, Japanese, or Korean. Asian-market sellers should skip it entirely.
- **Thin community footprint.** I searched r/ecommerce and related subs — almost no independent WiziShop threads. Far less community chatter than Shopify, which means fewer "someone else already hit this bug" war stories to learn from.
- **Digital products are awkward** (called out by resourcelinkai): the flow for virtual goods/downloads isn't smooth — physical products are its home turf.
- **Deep customization is limited:** you can edit HTML/CSS, but it's no WooCommerce-level open freedom.
